PixelPress HAP for Adobe Creative Cloud

Exports Hap, Hap Alpha, Hap Q, Hap Q Alpha, Hap Alpha-Only and Hap R (BC7) QuickTime
movies directly from the After Effects render queue, Premiere Pro and Adobe Media Encoder.

Runs natively on Windows (x64) and Apple Silicon Macs (M-series, macOS 11 or later).

In After Effects: Render Queue > Output Module > Format: "PixelPress HAP Movie",
then Format Options to choose Hap R (default) or another Hap variant.

Based on the HAP Encoder Plugin for Adobe CC by Greg Bakker and disguise technologies
limited (BSD licence), the Codec Foundation for Adobe CC, the Hap reference library by
Tom Butterworth / Vidvox, bc7e / bc7enc / rgbcx by Richard Geldreich, Jr., and FFmpeg
